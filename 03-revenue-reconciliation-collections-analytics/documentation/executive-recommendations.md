# Executive Recommendations

These are the two board-level recommendations proposed in the corrected executive brief (see [`../executive-brief/revenue-reconciliation-executive-brief.pdf`](../executive-brief/revenue-reconciliation-executive-brief.pdf)). They are recommendations, not confirmations that any action was taken, and not a guarantee of the stated impact.

## 1. Accelerate Collections on FY2025-26 Receivables

- **Target:** Recover the full $4.35M in outstanding FY2025-26 receivables by May 30, 2026.
- **Action:** Finance Collections contacts all FY2025-26 accounts with balances above $5,000 within seven days of the board meeting. The FY2023-24 outstanding balance of $1.53M — still unresolved two fiscal years after close — is escalated separately for write-off assessment or recovery action.
- **Impact:** Would close 100% of the current-year gap and address the largest aged variance in the three-year window before board close.
- **Owner:** Finance Collections Team.

Note the correction applied here relative to this project's original source materials: the FY2023-24 figure being escalated for recovery is the **$1.53M outstanding receivables** balance, not the $1.18M deferred-revenue figure for the same year — those are two distinct numbers that the original draft brief conflated. See [`../README.md`](../README.md#key-findings) for the full explanation.

## 2. Correct Revenue Attribution Before FY2026-27 Opens

- **Target:** Reclassify all Summer 2026 and Fall 2026 pre-payments from FY2025-26 income to Deferred Revenue by July 1, 2026.
- **Action:** One reclassification journal entry, plus a configuration change to source-system posting rules so fiscal-year allocation is applied at the point of transaction recording rather than by transaction date.
- **Impact:** Would produce accurate balance-sheet liability reporting and a clean opening position for FY2026-27 planning.
- **Owner:** CFO and IT Systems Team.

## What these recommendations don't claim

Neither recommendation has been implemented as part of this project — both are proposed actions for finance leadership to evaluate and execute. Recovery of the full $4.35M (or the $1.53M FY2023-24 balance) is not guaranteed; aged receivables of this kind often require partial write-off. This project analyzed, modeled, and flagged the underlying issues — it did not implement the fix or observe the outcome.
